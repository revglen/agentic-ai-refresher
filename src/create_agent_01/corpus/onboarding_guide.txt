New Hire Onboarding Guide (Sample Corpus Document)

Week one: complete identity verification, receive equipment, and finish
mandatory security awareness training. New hires are assigned a buddy
from their team for the first 30 days.

Week two: shadow at least two team meetings and complete role-specific
system access requests. Access to production systems requires a
separate approval step from the security team.

30-60-90: managers should hold a check-in at 30, 60, and 90 days to
review progress against the onboarding plan and set goals for the next
period.
