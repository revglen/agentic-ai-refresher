Expense Reimbursement Policy (Sample Corpus Document)

Employees may claim reimbursement for reasonable business expenses,
including travel, client meals, and approved software subscriptions.
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred,
with an itemized receipt attached.

Meal reimbursement is capped at 40 currency units per day while
travelling. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Air travel must be booked in
economy class unless the flight duration exceeds six hours, in which
case premium economy is permitted.

Approval: expenses under 100 currency units are auto-approved. Anything
above that requires sign-off from a direct manager before reimbursement
is processed.
