Remote Work Policy (Sample Corpus Document)

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager
approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, during
which employees should be reachable on chat and video.

Equipment: the company provides a laptop and a one-time home office
stipend of 300 currency units. Employees are responsible for maintaining
a stable internet connection.

Security: remote employees must use the company VPN when accessing
internal systems and must enable full-disk encryption on any device used
for work.
